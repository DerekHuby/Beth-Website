Why play, not talk

Children don't always have the words for what they're feeling — but they can show us through play. Play therapy uses toys, art, sand, stories and movement as a child's natural language, giving them a safe way to process worry, change, loss or big transitions at their own pace.

Sessions take place in a warm, consistent space where a child leads and I follow, gently reflecting back what I notice. Over time, this builds confidence, emotional regulation and a stronger sense of self — skills that carry into home, friendships and school.

I work with children referred for anxiety, low mood, bereavement, family change, school difficulties, or simply a period of feeling "stuck." Every child's process looks different, and that's exactly the point.

Ways we can work together

Sessions are tailored to your child and family — here's how a typical journey looks.

01

Initial consultation

A conversation with parents or carers (without the child present) to understand what's going on, agree goals, and answer any questions before sessions begin. Usually 50 minutes.

02

Individual play therapy sessions

Weekly 45-minute sessions in the playroom, where your child leads the play while I provide a safe, consistent, non-judgemental presence. Most children attend for a term or more, reviewed regularly.

03

Parent & carer reviews

Periodic check-ins to share gentle observations, celebrate progress, and adjust the approach together — always respecting your child's confidentiality within the playroom.

04

School & setting liaison

With your consent, I can liaise with teachers or SENCOs to support your child's wider wellbeing, and offer school-based sessions where this is the best fit.
